Why we do it

We love a job
done properly.

There’s real satisfaction in walking into a tired, scuffed room and leaving it calm, bright and finished to a standard you can be proud of.

That feeling is why we do this work. Painting and decorating is a craft, and the difference between an average job and a great one is in the details most people never notice: walls filled and sanded until they’re smooth, a crisp straight line where the wall meets the ceiling, woodwork prepared properly rather than glossed over. We take the time to get those details right, because they are what make a finish look good on day one and still look good years later.

Whether it’s a single bedroom, a hallway and stairs, a full house or a kitchen respray, we bring the same care to every job, and we don’t call it finished until we’d be happy to live with it ourselves.

What we stand for

Passion, quality
and happy customers.

Three things guide every job we take on, from the first visit to the final walk-round.

01

A passion for the craft

We’re decorators because we enjoy it. We take pride in transforming a room, and we care about the small things: the right paint for each surface, neat edges, smooth woodwork and a finish with no drips, patches or missed spots.

02

Quality you can see

Floors and furniture protected, cracks filled, surfaces sanded and primed, and quality trade paints applied in proper coats. No shortcuts, because shortcuts always show in the end, usually just after the decorator has left.

03

Customers who are happy

Honest advice, clear written quotes and a tidy, respectful team in your home. We keep you updated as the work goes on, and we check the finished job with you before we leave. Our 5.0 Google rating is something we work hard to keep.
