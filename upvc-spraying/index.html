How we work

A new look outside, without the upheaval.

We start by looking at your frames and talking through colours. Anthracite grey, black, chartwell green and cream are popular choices, and we can match most RAL colours. On the day, glass, seals, brickwork and paving are masked and protected, every surface is cleaned and keyed, and the colour is sprayed in thin, even coats. We spray in dry weather and let you know when windows and doors can be opened and closed as normal.

uPVC spraying FAQs.

Is uPVC spraying cheaper than new windows?+

In most cases, yes. You keep your existing frames, glass and seals and only pay for the preparation and spraying, so a respray usually costs a fraction of replacement and avoids the mess of taking windows out.

How long does sprayed uPVC last?+

With proper cleaning, keying and an adhesion primer, a paint made for uPVC gives a hard-wearing finish that stands up well to the Welsh weather. Most of the durability comes from the preparation, which is why we never skip it.

Can you spray woodgrain or cream uPVC?+

Yes. Woodgrain, cream and previously coloured frames can all be sprayed with the right preparation. We’ll look at your windows and doors and tell you honestly what will work.

Will spraying affect my window guarantee?+

It can. Some manufacturers’ guarantees don’t cover frames that have been painted or sprayed, so if your windows are still under guarantee it’s worth checking with your installer first.

Can you spray uPVC in winter?+

The coatings need dry weather and mild enough temperatures to cure properly, so we plan exterior spraying around the forecast. We’ll agree dates with you and move them if the weather turns.

Which areas do you cover?+

All of Cardiff, as well as Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Newport and the surrounding towns and villages.

Yes. Send us a few photos of your windows and doors through the form or by email, and we’ll give you a clear written quote with no obligation.
