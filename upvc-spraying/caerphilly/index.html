uPVC spraying in Caerphilly: FAQs.

Can you spray uPVC on a terrace that opens onto the street?+

Yes. We mask and protect everything around the frames, keep the pavement clear and plan the work so the house stays secure throughout.

Can you match my neighbour’s window colour?+

Usually, yes. Most RAL colours can be matched, so tell us the colour or show us a photo and we’ll confirm it in the quote.

Is uPVC spraying cheaper than new windows in Caerphilly?+

In most cases, yes. You keep your existing frames, glass and seals and only pay for the preparation and spraying, so a respray usually costs a fraction of replacement and avoids the mess of taking windows out.

Will spraying affect my window guarantee?+

It can. Some manufacturers’ guarantees don’t cover frames that have been painted or sprayed, so if your windows are still under guarantee, check with your installer first.

Do you provide free uPVC spraying quotes in Caerphilly?+

Yes. Send us a few photos of your windows and doors through the form or by email, and we’ll give you a clear written quote with no obligation.
