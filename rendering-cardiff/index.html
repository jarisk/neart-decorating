Good to know

Rendering FAQs.

Which render is best for my house?+

It depends on the wall, the look you want and how much upkeep you’re happy with. Sand and cement is traditional and is usually painted. Silicone renders and K Rend are through-coloured and need very little maintenance. We’ll look at the wall and explain the options in your quote.

Can you render over pebbledash?+

Often, yes. If the existing pebbledash is sound, it can be prepared and rendered over. Any loose or hollow areas are taken back first so the new render bonds properly.

Can you render in winter?+

Render needs dry weather and temperatures above freezing while it cures, so rendering is best planned for the warmer months. We’ll agree dates with you and keep an eye on the forecast.

Do I need planning permission to render my house?+

Rendering a house is often allowed without planning permission, but it can depend on the property, for example in conservation areas or on listed buildings. Check with your local council if you’re unsure.

Can you paint the render as well?+

Yes. Painting is our main trade, so we can quote for rendering and masonry painting together, along with fascias, soffits, sills and doors.

Which areas do you cover?+

All of Cardiff, as well as Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Newport and the surrounding towns and villages.
