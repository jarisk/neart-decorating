Before we begin

Service FAQs.

What is included in a decorating quote?+

Your written quote sets out the agreed surfaces, preparation and painting work. Any important assumptions or exclusions are made clear before booking.

Do you supply paint and materials?+

Yes. We can specify and supply professional-quality primers, paints and sundries as part of the project.

Can you help us choose colours and finishes?+

Yes. We can advise on colour flow, light, sheen and durability so each finish suits the room and how it is used.

How do you protect the property?+

We use appropriate coverings, masking and dust control, then keep the work area orderly throughout the job.
