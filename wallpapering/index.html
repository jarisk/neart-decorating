Wallpaper fitters in Cardiff

Paper that hangs
straight and stays put.

Good wallpapering is mostly preparation. Hung on uneven, dusty or flaky walls, even expensive paper shows every flaw and starts to lift at the seams.

We strip old paper, fill and sand the walls, and size or line them where needed before a single roll goes up. Then we plan where the pattern falls, so the joins line up across the room and around windows, doors and corners. Every job is priced in writing up front.

Good to know

Wallpapering FAQs.

How much does wallpapering cost in Cardiff?+

It depends on the size of the walls, the type of paper and how much preparation the walls need. Stripping old paper or lining first adds time, and a single feature wall is a much smaller job than a full room. We price every job with a free written quote, so you know the full cost before we start.

Do I need to buy the wallpaper?+

Most customers choose and buy their own paper, and we’ll tell you how many rolls to order based on the room size and the pattern repeat. Buy all your rolls with the same batch number, as the colour can vary slightly between batches.

Can you hang new wallpaper over old wallpaper?+

We don’t recommend it. New paper hung over old can bubble and lift as the old layer softens. We strip the old paper, repair the walls and line them if needed, so the new paper has a sound surface to bond to.

Do my walls need lining paper?+

Not always. Lining paper helps on older or uneven walls and under heavier or more expensive papers. We’ll look at your walls and tell you honestly whether it’s worth it.

Can you hang murals and large-pattern papers?+

Yes. Murals and large patterns need careful planning so the design sits in the right place on the wall, so we set out the drops before we start pasting.

Which areas do you cover?+

All of Cardiff, as well as Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Newport and the surrounding towns and villages.

Do you provide free quotes?+

Yes. Send us a few photos of the room and a link to the paper you’ve chosen through the form or by email, and we’ll give you a clear written quote with no obligation.
