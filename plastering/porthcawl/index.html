Plastering in Porthcawl: FAQs.

Can you plaster a holiday let between bookings?+

Yes. Tell us your booking gaps and we’ll plan the plastering and decorating so the property is ready again as quickly as possible.

Can you plaster over damp walls?+

Not until the cause is dealt with. We’ll look at the wall and tell you honestly if the damp needs fixing first, so the new plaster lasts.

How long before I can paint new plaster in Porthcawl?+

It depends on the thickness of the plaster, the temperature and ventilation, but a skim coat usually needs at least a week or two to dry. It should be an even, light colour all over before the mist coat goes on. We’ll advise on timing for your job.

Can you decorate after plastering?+

Yes, that’s our main trade. We can quote for plastering and decorating together and schedule the painting once the plaster is ready.
