Good to know

Kitchen respraying FAQs.

Is a respray cheaper than a new kitchen?+

In most cases, yes. You keep your units, worktops and appliances and only pay for the preparation and spraying, so a respray usually costs a fraction of a full replacement and causes far less disruption.

Which kitchens can be resprayed?+

Most solid wood, MDF and previously painted kitchens respray very well. Laminate and gloss doors can often be sprayed with the right preparation and primer. Vinyl-wrapped doors that are peeling usually need the wrap removing first. We’ll check your units and tell you honestly what will work.

How long does a kitchen respray take?+

It depends on the size of the kitchen and how many doors, drawers and panels there are. We’ll give you an expected timescale in your written quote and plan the work so you’re without a usable kitchen for as little time as possible.

How durable is a sprayed finish?+

With the right preparation and a hard-wearing paint system, a sprayed kitchen finish stands up well to everyday cooking and cleaning. Most of the durability comes from the preparation, which is why we never skip the cleaning, sanding and priming.

Can I choose any colour?+

Yes, you can choose the colour and sheen you want. We can talk through options with you and suggest colours that suit your worktops, floor and walls.

Which areas do you cover?+

All of Cardiff, as well as Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Newport and the surrounding towns and villages.

Do you provide free quotes?+

Yes. Send us a few photos of your kitchen through the form or by email and we’ll arrange the next step, then give you a clear written quote with no obligation.
