Good to know

Rendering in Llantrisant: FAQs.

Can you render a new extension?+

Yes. We render new extensions and can match the texture and colour to the existing house, then paint both so they look like one building.

Do you work on older properties?+

Yes. We assess older walls first, because some period properties have original lime render that needs a compatible repair. If a job needs a lime specialist, we’ll tell you.

Can you render in winter?+

Render needs dry weather and temperatures above freezing while it cures, so rendering is best planned for the warmer months. We’ll agree dates with you and keep an eye on the forecast.

Can you paint the render as well?+

Yes. Painting is our main trade, so we can quote for rendering and masonry painting together, along with fascias, soffits, sills and doors.

Do you provide free rendering quotes in Llantrisant?+

Yes. Tell us about the walls and what you’d like done and we’ll arrange the next step, then give you a clear written quote with no obligation.
