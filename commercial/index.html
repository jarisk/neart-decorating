Good to know

Commercial decorating FAQs.

Can you work around our opening hours?+

Yes. We plan the work around how your business runs, phasing it area by area so you can keep operating. Let us know your hours and any quieter periods when you ask for a quote and we’ll build the schedule around them.

Do you work for landlords and letting agents?+

Yes. We refresh rental properties between tenancies and decorate communal areas such as hallways and stairwells. We can quote for a single property or several.

Are you insured?+

Yes, we’re a fully insured team. If you need to see our insurance documents before work starts, just ask.

Which areas do you cover?+

All of Cardiff and the surrounding area, including Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly and Newport, as well as Swansea and West Wales.

Do you provide free quotes?+

Yes. Tell us about the premises and what needs doing and we’ll arrange the next step, then give you a clear written quote with no obligation.
