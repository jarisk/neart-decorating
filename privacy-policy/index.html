Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

1. Who We Are

Neart Decorating Ltd (company number 17456164) (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a painting and decorating company based at 10 Flemingston Rd, Barry CF62 4JH. Contact us at info@neartdecorating.co.uk or 029 2168 0320.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to provide when you request a quote or contact us by phone or email. This may include your name, phone number, email address, property or project details, and the contents of your message.

If you accept optional cookies, we may also receive technical and usage information such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, approximate location, referral source, and interactions with the website or our advertisements.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotations
  • To arrange, deliver and manage decorating work
  • To keep business, accounting and legal records
  • With your consent, to measure website use and advertising effectiveness
  • To protect the website from fraud, abuse and security threats

We do not sell your personal information or use your enquiry details for marketing without your consent.

4. Our Legal Bases

We rely on steps taken at your request before entering a contract, performance of a contract, our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and operating our business, compliance with legal obligations, and your consent for optional analytics and advertising technologies. You may withdraw cookie consent at any time using “Cookie settings” in the website footer.

5. Service Providers

We use trusted providers to operate the website and process data on our behalf, including Basin for form submissions, Cloudflare for hosting and security, Google for fonts, analytics and advertising services, and Microsoft for advertising measurement. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices and may process data outside the UK using approved safeguards.

6. How Long We Keep Data

We normally keep enquiry data for up to 12 months. Records relating to completed work may be kept for up to six years for accounting and legal purposes. Analytics retention is controlled through our provider settings. We delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed.

7. Your Rights

Under UK data-protection law, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; and request data portability. Contact us at info@neartdecorating.co.uk. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

8. Cookies

We use necessary technologies for security and your cookie preference. Optional analytics and advertising technologies are used only after you accept them. See our Cookie Policy for more information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will always appear on this page.
