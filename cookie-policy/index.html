1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include tags, pixels and local browser storage. They can help a site operate securely, remember your choices, measure visits and understand advertising performance.

2. How We Use Them

We use strictly necessary technologies for website security and to remember your cookie choice. With your permission, Google Tag Manager may load analytics and advertising technologies that help us understand how the site is used and whether our advertising is effective.
