Good to know

Exterior painting FAQs.

When is the best time of year to paint the outside of a house?+

Exterior paint needs dry weather and mild temperatures to cure properly, so the main season runs from spring to early autumn. We plan dates around the forecast and won’t paint onto damp walls.

Do you arrange the scaffolding?+

Yes. We organise the scaffolding for you and plan the work so it goes up once, even if the job includes walls, woodwork and render repairs.

How long does an exterior repaint take?+

It depends on the size of the house, the amount of preparation and the weather. We’ll give you an expected timescale in your written quote and keep you updated if rain changes the plan.

Can you paint render and pebbledash?+

Yes. We wash and treat the walls, repair cracks and loose areas, and use breathable masonry paint suited to the surface. If the render is badly damaged, we can quote to repair or re-render it first.

Do I need to be at home while you work?+

Usually not, as long as we have access to the outside of the property. For rental properties we can arrange access with your tenants directly.

Which areas do you cover?+

All of Cardiff, as well as Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Newport and the surrounding towns and villages.

Do you provide free quotes?+

Yes. Tell us about your house and what needs doing and we’ll arrange the next step, then give you a clear written quote with no obligation.
