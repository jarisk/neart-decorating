Good to know

Cardiff decorating FAQs.

Which parts of Cardiff do you cover?+

We work across Cardiff, including Canton, Pontcanna, Roath, Heath, Llandaff, Radyr, Rumney, Splott, Grangetown and surrounding neighbourhoods.

Are you based in Cardiff?+

Yes. We’re at 357 Newport Rd, Cardiff CF24 1RN, and most of our work is across the city and its suburbs.

Do you decorate older Victorian and Edwardian homes?+

Yes. Older Cardiff homes often have lime plaster, original mouldings and many layers of old paint. We repair and prepare these surfaces carefully and choose products that suit them.

Do you provide free quotes?+

Yes. Tell us about your project and we’ll arrange the best next step, then provide a clear written quote with no obligation.

Can you help with preparation and repairs?+

Yes. Proper preparation is central to our work. We assess the surfaces first and include the preparation needed for a clean, durable finish in your quote.

Do you decorate occupied homes?+

Yes. We work carefully in lived-in homes, protecting floors and furniture and keeping the space as tidy and usable as possible throughout the project.
