Good to know

Plastering FAQs.

How long before I can paint new plaster?+

It depends on the thickness of the plaster, the temperature and ventilation, but a skim coat usually needs at least a week or two to dry. It should be an even, light colour all over before the mist coat goes on. We’ll advise on timing for your job.

Can you plaster over artex?+

Usually, yes. Once we’ve checked the surface is sound, artex can be skimmed over for a smooth, flat finish. With older artex, skimming over it is generally a safer approach than scraping it off.

Can you decorate after plastering?+

Yes, that’s our main trade. We can quote for plastering and decorating together and schedule the painting once the plaster is ready.
