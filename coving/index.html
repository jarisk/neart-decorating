How we work

Neat corners, tidy home.

We start by looking at the room, talking through profiles and measuring up. On the day, floors and furniture are covered, old coving is removed if needed, and the wall and ceiling are prepared so the new coving sits tight. Every corner is mitred, every joint is filled and caulked, and the coving is painted so there are no visible gaps or lines. We clear up at the end of each day.

We fit coving right across Cardiff, from Roath and Canton to Whitchurch, Rhiwbina and Pontprennau, as well as Penarth and the Vale, and we’re based on Newport Road in CF24. Landlords welcome: new coving is a quick way to hide ceiling cracks and refresh a rental between tenancies.

Good to know

Coving FAQs.

How much does coving cost?+

It depends on how much coving the room needs, the profile and material, the number of corners and whether old coving has to come off first. Send us the room sizes or a few photos and we’ll give you a clear written quote.

Can you fit coving to uneven walls and ceilings?+

Yes. Most older walls and ceilings aren’t perfectly straight. We fix the coving securely, then fill and caulk the gaps so the finished line looks clean and even.

Should I choose plaster or polyurethane coving?+

Plaster suits period homes and gives a crisp, traditional look. Polyurethane is lighter, quicker to fit and less prone to cracking. We’ll recommend the best option for your room and your budget.

Can you match my existing coving?+

Often, yes. Many common profiles are still available. For older or unusual cornice we’ll look at what can be sourced or repaired and tell you honestly what will work.

Will new coving hide cracks between the wall and ceiling?+

Yes, that’s one of the main reasons people fit it. Coving covers the joint where cracks tend to appear, and we prepare the surfaces first so the finish stays neat.

Do you paint the coving too?+

Yes. We can paint the coving on its own or decorate the whole room, including the ceiling and walls, in the same visit.

Which areas do you cover?+

All of Cardiff, as well as Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Newport and the surrounding towns and villages.

Do you provide free quotes?+

Yes. Send us a few photos of the room through the form or by email and we’ll arrange the next step, then give you a clear written quote with no obligation.
