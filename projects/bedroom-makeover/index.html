The project

Warm, calm
and grown-up.

A deeper wall colour can make a bedroom feel cosy and finished, as long as it goes on evenly and the edges are crisp.

The bed and furniture were covered and protected throughout, and the walls were repainted from a pale neutral to a warm latte tone. The new colour sits beautifully with the sage fitted wardrobes, brass lighting and soft furnishings, set against the white ceiling, skirting and doors.
