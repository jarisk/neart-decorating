The project

A hallway that
sets the tone.

The hallway, stairs and landing are the first thing you see when you come home, and one of the hardest areas of a house to decorate well.

This home already had panelling up the stairs and around the hallway and landing, but it needed proper preparation and a finish to do it justice. We protected the carpets and floors, filled and sanded the walls and panelling, and set up a scaffold tower so the high stairwell wall could be reached safely and painted evenly.

The finished scheme pairs a warm stone tone on the panelling with soft off-white walls above the dado rail. The spindles, handrails and newel posts went from white to a deep charcoal, which now frames the staircase, and the doors, frames and skirting were finished in crisp white.
