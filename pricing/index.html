How we price

A fair price for
a job done properly.

We don’t do vague estimates or surprise extras. You get a clear, detailed written quote, so you know exactly what you’re paying for before we start.

Our price reflects the time it takes to do the job well: protecting your floors and furniture, filling and sanding, preparing woodwork properly, and applying quality paints in proper coats. The things that change the price most are the size of the area, the condition of the surfaces, ceiling heights and stairwells, the amount of woodwork, any colour changes and the access needed.

Pricing questions

Why are your prices shown as “from”?+

Every property is different. The size of the rooms, the condition of the walls and woodwork, ceiling heights and access all change the amount of work involved, so our guide prices show where a typical job starts. Your written quote gives the exact price for your home.

Are quotes free?+

Yes. Tell us about your project and we’ll arrange the next step, then give you a clear written quote with no obligation.

How quickly will I get a quote?+

We respond to enquiries within 30–60 minutes during working hours, and our written quotes are turned around within 24 hours.

Can you price a job from photos?+

For many jobs, such as kitchen respraying, uPVC spraying and wallpapering, a few clear photos and some measurements are enough for us to give you a written quote. For larger decorating jobs we’ll usually arrange a visit.

Are there any hidden costs?+

No. Our quotes are detailed and in writing, so you know what is included and what it costs before any work starts.
