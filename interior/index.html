Good to know

Interior painting FAQs.

Do I need to move my furniture before you start?+

No. We move furniture away from the walls and cover it, and protect floors with dust sheets. If there are fragile or valuable items, it helps to put those away before we arrive.

How long does it take to decorate a room?+

It depends on the size of the room and how much preparation the walls and woodwork need. We’ll give you an expected timescale in your written quote, and for larger jobs we work through the house room by room.

Can you paint new plaster?+

Yes. New plaster needs to dry out fully and then have a mist coat before the finish coats go on. We’ll check it’s ready and prepare it properly so the paint doesn’t flake.

Can I stay at home while you decorate?+

Yes, most of our customers do. We plan the work so you always have rooms to use and leave everything tidy at the end of each day.

Do you work for landlords and letting agents?+

Yes. We refresh rental properties between tenancies and can arrange access with tenants or agents directly.

Which areas do you cover?+

All of Cardiff, as well as Penarth, Barry, Caerphilly, Newport and the surrounding towns and villages.

Do you provide free quotes?+

Yes. Tell us about the rooms and what needs doing and we’ll arrange the next step, then give you a clear written quote with no obligation.
