Good to know

Painting & decorating
FAQs.

How much does a painter and decorator cost in Cardiff?+

Most interior work starts from £12 per m², which is around £900 for a 1-bed flat, £1,500 for a 2-bed home and £2,500 for a 3-bed home. Exterior painting starts from £25 per m². Your exact price depends on the condition of the surfaces, room sizes and finishes, so we always give a free written quote after seeing the property.

Do you charge a day rate?+

No. We price every job with a detailed written quote, so you know the full cost before we start and it doesn’t grow if the work takes longer than planned.

How long does it take to paint a 3-bed house?+

It depends on how many rooms are included and how much preparation the walls and woodwork need. We’ll give you a clear timeframe with your quote, so you can plan around the work.

Do you supply the paint?+

Yes. We supply trusted, high-quality trade paints and materials and include them in your quote. If you’ve already chosen a brand or colour, we’re happy to use it.

Do you work in occupied homes?+

Yes. We protect floors and furniture, keep the work area organised and tidy up at the end of every day, so your home stays as usable as possible while we work.

Can you decorate rental or empty properties?+

Yes. We work for landlords, letting agents and property managers on end-of-tenancy repaints, empty-property turnarounds and communal areas, planned around your tenancy dates.

Are you insured?+

Yes. Neart Decorating is a fully insured painting and decorating company.

Which areas do you cover?+

We work across Cardiff, including Roath, Canton, Pontcanna, Llandaff, Whitchurch, Penylan and Cardiff Bay, plus Penarth, Barry, Newport, Caerphilly and other nearby towns. See all the areas we cover.
